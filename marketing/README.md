## 💡 Pro Tips

### Instagram:
✅ **Post at Peak Times**: 10 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM IST
✅ **Use All Features**: Feed, Stories, Reels, Carousels
✅ **Engage Within 1 Hour**: Respond to comments immediately
✅ **Save Top Content**: Organize Highlights for Stories
✅ **Collaborate**: Partner with micro-influencers (1k-10k followers)

### LinkedIn:
✅ **Be Professional**: Less casual than Instagram
✅ **Data-Driven**: Include statistics and metrics
✅ **Long-Form**: Don't be afraid of 1000+ word posts
✅ **Engage Actively**: Comment on others' posts daily
✅ **Build Relationships**: Connect genuinely, not just for reach

### Videos:
✅ **Hook in 3 Seconds**: Capture attention immediately
✅ **Captions Always**: 85% watch without sound
✅ **Keep It Short**: 15-60 seconds ideal for social
✅ **Clear CTA**: Tell viewers what to do next
✅ **Test Thumbnails**: A/B test different covers

### General:
✅ **Consistency > Perfection**: Post regularly, even if not perfect
✅ **Quality > Quantity**: 3 great posts better than 7 mediocre ones
✅ **Listen to Audience**: Create what they engage with most
✅ **Analyze Data**: Weekly analytics review is crucial
✅ **Stay Authentic**: Be genuine, not salesy
